Resources

The stuff nobody hands you.

Grants, scholarships, festivals, internships and practical tips, filtered for people still in high school. New finds go on our Instagram first; the keepers get collected here.

Grants

Real money and gear for student films. Two worth knowing: the Panavision New Filmmaker Program (camera equipment grants for student filmmakers shooting real projects - panavision.com) and the Carole Dorothy Joyce Student Filmmaker Grant (an annual grant from the Texas Film Commission, for Texas residents - gov.texas.gov). We post new ones on Instagram as they open.

Scholarships & awards

The National YoungArts Foundation competition (film, ages 15 to 18) comes with cash awards, mentorship and national recognition - youngarts.org. We keep a running list of scholarships actually open to high school filmmakers and post them as they open.

Festivals

Plenty of regional and student festivals have high school categories with real audiences and real laurels. We post the ones with open submissions and sane entry fees. Your film stays yours everywhere we point you. All 22 we have vetted so far are on the festival map.

Internships & programs

Programs that actually take high schoolers: Fresh Films (a paid summer film program and weekly filmmaking for teens, you crew real shoots - freshfilms.org) and Reel Works (free after-school filmmaking with industry mentors for NYC teens - reelworks.org). We track internships and summer intensives as they open and post them on Instagram first.

Tips

Free, practical posts on shooting, lighting, sound, directing actors and editing, written for people working with no budget and a borrowed camera. They start as carousels on Instagram; the best ones get expanded here.

Something we missed?

Found a program, internship or festival that was good to you? DM it to @highschoolfilmmakers and we'll share it with the network.
