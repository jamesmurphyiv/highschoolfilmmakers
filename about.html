About

What this is.

High School Filmmakers is a network for students making movies right now. Not someday. Now.

The network

Every week we feature one high school filmmaker and share their work with the whole network. Alongside that, we post free, practical tips on shooting, lighting, festivals and everything else nobody teaches you.

How to join

Follow @highschoolfilmmakers and submit your work. That's it. If your work gets featured, it's always credited to you and it always stays yours.
