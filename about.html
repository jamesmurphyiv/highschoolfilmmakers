About

What this is.

High School Filmmakers is a network for students making movies right now. Not someday. Now.

The network

Most high school filmmakers are the only one at their school. HSF exists to fix that. We connect you with actors, crew, editors and composers your age, and we track the internships, programs, festivals and tips that help you make the next thing better.

What this is not

We're not a festival, a distributor or a competition. Nobody here is judging your film or charging you to be seen. The point is the people: once you have a crew, everything else follows.

How to join

Follow @highschoolfilmmakers. That's it. When you need someone, or you want to be someone else's missing piece, DM us. We never ask for or store your personal information; everything runs through Instagram.
