Find your people

Nobody makes a movie alone.

Actors, DPs, editors, sound, a whole crew - whatever your film is missing, someone your age already has it. Two ways to connect.

The fast way

The Board.

Post your project with the roles you need, or browse projects near you and tap a role to sign up. One tap, your first name and age range, done - the project owner sees you on their private responses page. Every post is reviewed by a human before it goes live.

Browse the board Post a project

How it works

Three steps to a full crew.

1. Post or browse. Making something? List the roles you need. Looking for a set? Filter by state and role.

2. Tap a role. Signing up takes ten seconds. No accounts, no forms beyond a first name and age range.

3. Make the movie. The project owner checks their responses, reaches out on Instagram if you left a handle, and you take it from there.

The personal way

DM us and we boost it.

Message @highschoolfilmmakers with what you're making, who you need and where you're based. We put it in front of the whole network. Want to be found instead? Send your skills and your state, and we'll point matching projects at you.

Stay safe

We never ask for your email or phone number, and you should never post yours. Location on the board is city-level only. Posts are reviewed before they go up, sign-ups are structured (no open text), and every post has a report button. Meeting someone about a project? Keep chats on the platform, meet in a group, and loop in a parent or guardian. The full rules live in the Terms & Conditions.
