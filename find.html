Find your people

Nobody makes a movie alone.

Whatever you're missing, someone in the network has it. Here's how to get connected.

Step 1

DM us what you need.

Message @highschoolfilmmakers on Instagram with three things: what you're making, who you're looking for (an actor, a DP, an editor, a whole crew), and where you're based.

Step 2

We put it in front of the network.

Your call goes out to everyone following HSF, the actors, crew and editors looking for exactly this. Need to stay low-key? Say so and we'll post it without your handle.

Step 3

They come to you.

People who fit reply to you directly on Instagram. You check their work, you pick, you make the movie. We stay out of it.

Want to be found?

Flip it. DM us your skills (camera, editing, acting, sound, whatever) and where you are. When a matching project comes through, we point them at you.

The fine print

Everything happens on Instagram, in public or in your own DMs. We don't run forms, make accounts or keep your information. You're always talking to people your own age making the same kind of thing, but use the same judgment you'd use meeting anyone online: keep chats on the platform, meet in a group, and loop in a parent or guardian for anything in person.
